5. Methodological Scope, Controls & Scientific Limitations (v1.2)

  • Exploratory Transcriptomic & Survival Screen: Across 3,492 strongly upregulated protein-coding genes (log2FC > 1.5, DESeq2 FDR < 0.01), median-split Kaplan–Meier log-rank testing identified 458 nominal associations (p < 0.05) and 183 candidates at Benjamini–Hochberg FDR q < 0.10 (HR > 1.0), alongside complete-case multivariable Cox regression adjusted for age, sex, and pathological stage. These represent exploratory survival associations rather than validated clinical biomarkers.
  • STRING v12 Functional Association Network: Network degree and betweenness centrality quantify functional connectivity (combined confidence score ≥ 0.70, encompassing physical and pathway associations) rather than direct physical binding strength or standalone therapeutic druggability.
  • Positive Control Redocking Audit (PLK1 2RKU + (7R)-BI-2536): Co-crystal redocking with stereochemically verified (7R)-BI-2536 yielded a symmetry-aware heavy-atom RMSD of Did not pass (5.556 Å > 2.00 Å; score -9.419) — flexible tail shift in rigid receptor. Because this exceeds the standard 2.00 Å pose-recovery benchmark due to flexible tail movement in a rigid receptor, all Vina scores are interpreted strictly as hypothesis-generating rankings rather than validated binding affinities (ΔG).
  • Mechanism-Aware Comparator Separation: Rigosertib is flagged as a non-ATP-competitive modulator and excluded from the ATP-pocket selectivity ranking (displayed separately as an external comparator).
